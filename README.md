## About

I'm a Certified Bubble.io Developer and UI/UX Designer with approximately eight years of commercial Bubble.io and digital product development experience. My work spans SaaS products, CRMs, marketplaces, internal tools and multi-role applications.

I bring practical experience with REST APIs, OAuth, webhooks, backend workflows and database structures, including integrations with Stripe, PandaDoc, SendGrid and other business services. My background also includes QA, troubleshooting, product architecture and guiding development teams.

I'm expanding into AI-powered workflow automation through Make.com, n8n, LLM integrations and JavaScript/TypeScript. My focus is reliable, maintainable business workflows with clear data boundaries, explicit decision rules and useful failure handling.
